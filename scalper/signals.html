How to place the orders on Delta

  1. Place each position's orders in the order listed: the protective BUY legs first, then the SELL legs, so a sold option is never left uncovered.
  2. Use limit orders. Sell at the limit shown or higher (it is 96% of Delta's mark); buy at the limit shown or lower (104% of mark). If a leg doesn't fill in ~5 minutes, move it closer to the mark; never chase further than the limit.
  3. If a SELL leg cannot be filled, leave the bought protection (it costs little) and skip that position this week.
  4. Exit: nothing to do. Every position is held to expiry and Delta settles it automatically at 17:30 IST (Saturday or Sunday). Do not close early: the tested results hold to expiry.
