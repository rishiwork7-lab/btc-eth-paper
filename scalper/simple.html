How Our Strategies Make Money (In Plain English)

No quant jargon. Here is how the system works and why your capital is protected.

1. Why do we trade only on Friday nights?

Over the weekend, institutions stop trading and crypto volatility drastically drops. However, retail option buyers continue to overpay for crash protection insurance. Every Friday at 23:30 IST, our strategies sell this expensive insurance and buy cheap protective wings further out.

2. How is my capital protected from a crash?

We never sell uncovered options. Every single trade buys outer "wings" that act as a 100% hard stop-loss. Even if Bitcoin or Ethereum drops 50% in an hour, your loss is capped before the order is placed. The worst-case loss is fixed and known in advance.

3. What does the "25% safety cap" mean?

The system has a built-in risk manager. Across all positions combined, contract sizes are automatically scaled down so that even in the most catastrophic market weekend imaginable, the maximum theoretical loss across everything cannot exceed 25% of your account.

4. When do trades finish and settle?

Saturday night condors settle on Saturday at 17:30 IST. Sunday trend spreads settle on Sunday at 17:30 IST. Delta Exchange settles them automatically at their 30-minute index average. You never have to close them manually.
