The rule (fixed before testing; nothing tuned on the result)

  1. Before each candle opens, a buy-stop sits at the previous candle's high and a sell-stop at its low.
  2. If the candle trades above the previous high → long there; below the previous low → short there. If it opens beyond a level, the fill is the open.
  3. Book the trade on the next candle: exit at the close of the next candle. One trade at a time.
  4. A candle that breaks both levels is skipped: 1-minute candles cannot tell which break came first.
